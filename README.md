About Me
I'm a Houston-based AI enthusiast currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in AI and Robotics. My background is in logistics. I spent years working in operations at FedEx, where I earned the Purple Promise Award for service excellence. That experience taught me how to solve real problems efficiently, and it is the same mindset I bring to everything I do in AI.
I own and operate Supreme Moon AI Consulting, a Houston-based AI consulting agency where I help local small businesses use AI in practical, impactful ways. From creating marketing content to automating everyday tasks, I work directly with business owners to show them how AI can grow their brand and increase revenue without needing a technical background. Seeing a small business owner use AI confidently for the first time is one of the most rewarding parts of what I do.
In addition to running my agency, I do contract work for several AI companies where I contribute to improving AI projects and helping teams build better, more accurate models. This hands-on work keeps me sharp and connected to where the field is actually heading.
My interest in machine learning grew out of a simple question: how do machines learn to see, understand, and decide? The more I explored it, the more I realized this field sits at the intersection of everything I care about. Problem-solving, pattern recognition, and building things that actually work in the real world. Whether it is training a neural network, fine-tuning a language model, or experimenting with image generation, I am always looking for ways to connect the theory to something tangible.
This repository is a living record of that journey.
